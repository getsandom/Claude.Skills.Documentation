# Decision template

```markdown
# DEC_NNN: Title phrased as a statement

- **Updated:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Related:** links to decisions, issues, improvements and architecture

## Context

The problem or question that required the decision, and the relevant constraints.
Written for someone who did not take part in the discussion.

## Decision

What was decided, in a direct and verifiable way. Concrete rules (codes,
limits, names) as a list.

## Alternatives considered

- **Alternative A:** in one or two sentences, and why it was discarded.
- **Alternative B:** ...

## Consequences

- What becomes true or mandatory in the system.
- Accepted costs, risks and limitations.
- Work the decision creates (with a link to issues, if any).
```
