# Rules: decisions

- **Identifier:** `DEC_NNN`
- **Template:** [template.md](template.md)
- **File name:** `DEC_NNN-short-title.md` (e.g. `DEC_004-idempotency.md`)
- **Index description:** what was decided
- **On closing:** remove the file and move the line to the Closed section of the index, with the outcome Revoked or Superseded by `DEC_NNN`
- **Subtopics:** none

## What to record

A decision records a choice already made about the behavior, the contract or the
structure of the project, together with the reason and what was discarded. Record
here only what was actually decided: topics without a conclusion go to the issues;
ideas for the future, to the improvements.

## Lifecycle

- One decision per file.
- If the decision changes, edit the file to reflect what applies now and record the
  change in the history.
- If it is revoked or superseded, remove the file, move its line to the Closed
  section of the index and record it in the history.
